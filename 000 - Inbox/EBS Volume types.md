They Are 6 types
1. gp2/gp3 (SSD): General Purpose SSD that balance price and performance for a variety of workloads
2. io1/io2 Block Express (SSD): Highest performance
3. st1 (HDD): low cost hdd vol, for frequently accessed data
4. sc1 (HDD): lowest cost for less frequent accessed data

EBS Vols are characterized based on Size, Throughput, and IOPS
